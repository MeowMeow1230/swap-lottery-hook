Why Existing Solutions Don't Work

Traders flow through liquidity pools like water — they come, swap, and never return.

Static LP Fees

Passive income with zero incentive for traders to return. Pools bleed volume to competitors with better UX. No loyalty mechanism exists.

Points & Blind Mining

Delayed gratification over 3–6 month vesting cycles. User fatigue from over-promised airdrops. Traders are tired of being farmed.

Dynamic Fee Hooks

Only protect LPs by raising fees during volatility — driving traders AWAY. Solving one side at the expense of the other is not a solution.

We need a mechanism that rewards traders for returning — without penalizing LPs.
